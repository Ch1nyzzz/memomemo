\subsection{Supplementary related work}
\label{app:supp_related}

\paragraph{Prompt and context optimization.}
Beyond the optimization systems most directly related to \name, several
methods search over prompts or agent context. EvoPrompt~\cite{guo2024evoprompt}
couples evolutionary operators with LLM mutation, while
DSPy~\cite{khattab2023dspy} compiles declarative module signatures into
optimized few-shot chains. ACE~\cite{zhang2025ace} evolves system prompts and
memory playbooks through generation, reflection, and curation stages. These
methods motivate treating context as an optimization target, but they do not
address the file-backed, multi-iteration workspace exposed to coding-agent
proposers.

\paragraph{Evolutionary and program search.}
FunSearch~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch} pairs an LLM with island-based
evolution to discover programs under automated fitness functions.
AlphaEvolve~\cite{novikov2025alphaevolve} extends this style of search to
multi-file codebases with a coding-agent proposer, and
OpenEvolve~\cite{sharma2025openevolve} provides an open implementation.
ELM~\cite{lehman2023elm} formalizes LLMs as general mutation operators within
evolutionary computation. These systems motivate LLM-based program search, but
they maintain populations or evolutionary selection pressure over programs. In
contrast, \name optimizes agent harnesses, maintains a Pareto frontier of
candidates, and studies how to curate the filesystem evidence read by a single
coding-agent proposer.

\paragraph{LLM agents and technical foundations.}
Modern LLM agents such as MemGPT~\cite{packer2023memgpt},
Reflexion~\cite{shinn2023reflexion}, Voyager~\cite{wang2023voyager}, and
SWE-agent~\cite{yang2024sweagent} are optimization targets or benchmark
scaffolds rather than competing optimizers in our experiments. On the
algorithmic side, our file-level policy draws on UCB1~\cite{auer2002ucb}: each
workspace file is treated as an arm, reward is computed from recent harness
performance, and an exploration bonus prevents premature convergence.
More broadly, the work connects to AutoML~\cite{he2021automl}, with a search
space defined by natural-language code edits rather than a fixed
configuration grammar.
